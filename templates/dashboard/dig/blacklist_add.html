Blacklist Policy
Blacklisting is a command-level enforcement action. Only add entries backed by a verifiable report or investigation.
  • Immediate effect. Entries apply as soon as they are saved - no further approval step is required.
  • Re-adding updates. Submitting a value that is already blacklisted updates the existing entry's reason and actor.
  • Automatic blacklisting. The system can auto-blacklist a plate once it accumulates several unpaid challans (threshold configured by IT Support under System Settings).
  • Audit trail. Every add or update is recorded in the audit log with your account, the value and the reason.
  • Supported types. Vehicle plates and CNIC numbers. Phone / chassis blacklist entries are not enabled yet.
What happens next
  1. The entry is saved to the blacklist register.
  2. Future detections matching the value are surfaced for priority action.
  3. The register shows the entry with your name, timestamp and reason.
